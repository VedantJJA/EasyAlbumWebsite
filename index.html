EasyAlbum is an Android app for sharing photos with the people you choose. Create an album, link a Google Drive for storage, and give friends and family access.

See how it works ↓

How EasyAlbum works

Organize your photos

Create albums and folders. Browse photos by date and open an image to zoom, check its details, or edit it if you have permission.

Choose who has access

Invite viewers or members by EasyAlbum username or a selected contact email. Members can also add photos and download originals.

Choose where photos go

Connect one or more Drives, see available storage, and select a destination for each batch. Photos are stored in EasyAlbum’s hidden Google Drive app-data folder.
